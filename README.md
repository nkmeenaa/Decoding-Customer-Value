# Customer Purchasing Behavior Analysis

## 📋 Project Overview
This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior using a retail dataset of 3,900 customer records. 
* **Objective:** Identify high-value customers, understand loyalty patterns, evaluate promotional dependency, and generate actionable business recommendations.
* **Tools Used:** Python, MySQL, and Power BI.

## 🛠️ Data Preparation
Data cleaning and preprocessing were performed to handle missing values and prepare the dataset for analysis. The following business-focused features were engineered to improve customer segmentation:
* **Customer Value Score**
* **Loyalty Score**
* **Satisfaction Flag**
* **Promotional Dependency Level**

## 📊 Key Findings
* **Revenue by Category:** Clothing is the highest revenue-generating category ($104,264), followed by Accessories.
* **Revenue by Demographics:** Male customers generated a larger share of total revenue compared to female customers.
* **Seasonal Demand:** Revenue remained relatively stable across seasons, indicating consistent demand throughout the year.

## 👥 Customer Segmentation & Loyalty
* **Value Segments:** Medium Value customers represent the largest group, while High Value customers contribute significantly higher average purchase amounts.
* **Loyalty:** Over 1,000 highly loyal customers were identified, though nearly half of the customer base falls into medium loyalty segments.
* **Satisfaction:** A considerable portion of customers were classified as unsatisfied based on review ratings, highlighting opportunities for service improvement.

## 🏷️ Promotional Dependency
Approximately **43% of customers are highly dependent on discounts** and promotional offers. While promotions drive purchases, excessive reliance on them may affect long-term profitability.

## ⭐ Ideal Customer Profile
An ideal customer segment was identified by combining **High Value**, **High Loyalty**, and **Low Promotional Dependency**. A total of 356 customers matched these criteria, representing the most valuable group for retention strategies.

## 🚀 Recommendations
To improve customer retention, increase revenue, and support sustainable growth, businesses should focus on:
1. Retaining high-value customers and expanding loyalty programs.
2. Improving customer satisfaction to address the high rate of unsatisfied reviews.
3. Reducing dependence on promotional discounts.
4. Investing more heavily in high-performing categories like Clothing and Accessories.
